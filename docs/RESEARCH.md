## 1. What changed the plan (top findings)

1. **The public job posting has a different stack.** LinkedIn (May 2026, now closed): Next.js 15 · React 19 · TypeScript · Supabase · Tailwind · Radix · Stripe · Resend · Twilio · Sentry · Netlify · Anthropic SDK. No NestJS, Redis, BullMQ or QuickBooks. "Senior Full Stack / AI-First" and "Spade AI" appear nowhere public. → **Unresolved fork S1/S2** (B §0–1, H #3).
2. **She already shipped the product.** `portal.soloselect.com` is live (Next.js, Stripe, Sentry) with "Ask Spade" BETA answering customer questions. She builds the frontend herself ("wires it up 80–85%") and wants a **backend owner**. Our tagline paraphrased her copy. (B §0.3, §2.1; H #4)
3. **She wants receipts, not a landing page.** "Show us what you've built. Live links, repos, shipped work." (B §1.1; H #5)
4. **Firm requirements: hands-on equine experience + North Texas.** Not a code problem; the cover email must lead with the truth. (B §1.2; H #1)
5. **The role may be closed** (careers page empty, LinkedIn closed). Send a four-line check to mel@soloselect.com CC careers@ on day 0; build anyway. (B §0.6; H #2)
6. **ICSI fertilization is outsourced** to Genetech, Purcell OK (Monday aspiration → count back day 7–10). The in-house USDA lab is diagnostics only. (A §2, §3.2)
7. **Billing is triggered by clinical milestones**: day-24 heartbeat → $5,000/$6,500 lease + $22/day board; 45–60 days → ICSI stallion fee; 55 days → Select Genes; live foal; Dec 1 recip return or $6,000. (A §3.3–3.4)
8. **Embryo intake is SMS** ("a text is not confirmed until we reply"); semen orders by call/text before 5 PM, cancel by 8 AM; 100–150 orders/day in season. (A §3.1, §3.3)
9. **Status to owners = group texts + hand-built PDF lists** several times a week. That is the thing to replace. (A §6, B §3.5)
10. **The auction runs on Auction Mobility** (vendor SaaS). Stripe/QBO are not public — label as assumptions. (A §10.2, §5.10)
11. **Their site is Next.js + Tailwind v4 on Vercel.** Measured maroon `#600312`; Montserrat / Inter / Oswald. Light, white pages; no gradients. (G §1)
12. **Architecture theater confirmed:** NestJS, Redis (local Redis is 3.0 — BullMQ refuses it), pgvector, Auth.js v5 (maintenance mode), Turborepo, Promptfoo, three.js (~250 KB gz; R3F caps React <19.3). (D §6, E §4.2, G3)
