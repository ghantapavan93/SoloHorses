- **FACT** Transfer window "February 1 through July 15, 2026." Donor work "Quote upon request."
- **FACT** ICSI steps: OPU on sedated mare → oocytes packed in medium → **shipped to Genetech** → culture "roughly seven to ten days" → transfer fresh or vitrify → recipient match → heartbeat confirmation. Ep.12: "we aspirate every Monday. ## 10. CRITIC — where the existing plan is wrong or naive about Solo

2. **The sale is not "their own platform" in the software sense.** bid.soloselecthorses.com is Auction Mobility (vendor SaaS, white-label app, its own invoicing/payment processing) under a separate LLC (Select Horse Sales). The About page's "built proprietary sales platform" (2020) is a business claim, not a codebase.
